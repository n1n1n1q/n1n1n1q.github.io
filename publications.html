Neural Rabbit: High-Fidelity 3D Surface Reconstruction using Gaussian Splatting
CVPR 2026
Oleh Basystyi, Co-Author One, Co-Author Two

We introduce a novel surface-aligned Gaussian splatting representation that reconstructs smooth, high-fidelity 3D meshes of complex geometries from sparse-view photos in real-time.
