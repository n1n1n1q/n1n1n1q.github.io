Neural Rabbit: High-Fidelity 3D Surface Reconstruction using Gaussian Splatting

Oleh Basystyi1, Vladyslav Speedorak1, Anna Sus2

1Ukrainian Catholic University 2Collaborating Institution

* Equal contribution · † Corresponding author

Paper arXiv Code Video

Abstract

We introduce a novel surface-aligned Gaussian splatting representation that reconstructs smooth, high-fidelity 3D meshes of complex geometries from sparse-view photos in real-time. By enforcing geometric constraints directly on the 3D Gaussians, we bridge the gap between radiant neural rendering and traditional explicit triangle meshes.

Method

Our pipeline optimizes Gaussian parameters with an explicit depth consistency term and normal regularization. This aligns the flat Gaussian splats along the local tangent plane of the underlying geometry.

Once optimized, we extract a watertight triangle mesh using Poisson reconstruction, yielding high-fidelity surfaces with crisp details.

Results

Baseline point cloud against our surface mesh on the same scene, followed by more reconstructions from our test set.
